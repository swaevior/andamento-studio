Emblema engagements

In a Roman mosaic, the emblema was the centrepiece: made by the master's own hand, then set into the floor. An Emblema engagement is that kind of work. Senior, personal, never passed down the line.

Engagements are scoped privately after a discovery session. We don't publish rates.

Fees on request, for those who don't need to ask.
  1. i

    Discovery session

    A focused conversation about where the business is, where it's stuck, and whether we're the right fit.

  2. ii

    A written scope

    What will be done, by when, and what done looks like. Plain language, no surprises.

  3. iii

    The work, by hand

    Done by the principal, start to finish, with every decision written down.

The name

Why andamento

In mosaic-making, the andamento is the flow of the rows, the line the pieces follow that gives the whole picture its movement. A working life can have one too. These are some of its pieces.

  1. 2010

    Fellow at a Tibetan Buddhist monastery

    In India, at the end of a long overland tour of Asia. A lesson in patience that never wore off.

  2. 2013

    Teacher

    English lessons for migrant farmworkers and their families, through the University of Michigan.

  3. 2016–2020

    Co-founder, President & CFO

    Gamer Ammo, a gaming and blockchain startup building Clutch, a chat platform to help gamers earn from play. The 30-person company's payroll, compliance and books.

  4. Publisher

    The Sexy Politico

    An independent publication and community for discussing emerging and crucial issues, at home and abroad.

  5. 2019 on

    Consultant & publisher

    Small-business strategy, relief programs, contracts, and four books taken to print.

  6. 2020 on

    Disaster-relief volunteer

    With Team Rubicon, and in public-health preparedness through the pandemic.

  7. 2022

    Linguist

    BA in Linguistics, University of Michigan. Spanish, fluently.

  8. 2022 on

    Security & dispatch

    Site security and event dispatch: plan for what can go wrong, write it all down, stay calm.

  9. 2026

    Founder of Andamento

    Every piece, finally pointed the same way.

The principal

swae

sb swae · Founder & Principal

sb swae is the Founder & Principal of Andamento Studio. Fiercely independent by temperament, swae has lived several working lives: a fellowship at a Tibetan Buddhist monastery in India, publishing The Sexy Politico, an independent political publication, co-founding the gaming and blockchain startup Gamer Ammo as its President and CFO, consulting for small businesses through the COVID relief programs, and bringing four books to print. Years in security operations and event dispatch built the habits the studio runs on: plan for what can go wrong, write everything down, and stay calm when things move fast. A University of Michigan linguistics graduate, swae speaks Spanish fluently and volunteers in disaster relief with Team Rubicon.
